# MACRO RATINGS (round 8)

Every macro of the twelve instruments, for you to rate **by ear**. The next round works only on what you flag here.

How to fill it in: on SOUND, load the instrument's first preset, turn the macro from 0 to 100 while a part plays, and put
**S** (too subtle), **G** (good) or **M** (too much) in the RATING column; a word in NOTES if it helps ("dead in the
first half", "jumps at 70", "wrong job").

The CHANGE column is what the host measured: the round 6 audit's distance between the sound at 0 and at 100 (brightness,
its change, attack, sustain, motion, level; two presets nearer than 0.60 count as the same sound). Before = before this
round's widening. Round 8 widened every continuous macro under **0.40** (the lowest fifth of the table) and gave
three dead ones a job: TINE's and GLINT's DECAY and GLINT's ATTACK moved the track's ADSR, which FM6 does not use (it
plays its own operator envelopes), so they did nothing since round 1; they are GRIT (TINE), TREM and CHORUS (GLINT) now.
GLIDE, SLIDE and VIB move pitch between and around notes; the measuring phrase hears neither, so their numbers mean
nothing (they were widened anyway). SWITCH macros step through named values; MORPH is macro 1, JAM's knob.
